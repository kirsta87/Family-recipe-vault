Recipe Vault v1.5.0 — PDF Cookbook Library

- Adds a dedicated cookbook shelf to the Recipe Vault.
- Upload and analyze purchased cookbook PDFs in the browser.
- Detects likely recipes while skipping low-confidence front matter and index pages.
- Review, edit, select, and import detected recipes before anything is saved.
- Stores cookbook title, page number, author, cover preview, and default collection.
- Browse and search recipes inside an individual cookbook.
- Imported recipes remain available throughout search, Inspire Me, meal planning, pantry, and shopping.
- Cookbook removal includes shelf-only and cookbook-plus-recipes choices.

Important: PDF layouts vary. The importer intentionally uses a review queue instead of silently importing uncertain text.
