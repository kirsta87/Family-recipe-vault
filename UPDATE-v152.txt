Recipe Vault v1.5.2 — Cookbook Photo Scan Reliability

- Adds a hard timeout for each recipe photo scan so a PDF image object can never freeze the whole cookbook import.
- Adds a shorter timeout for PDF.js image-object callbacks that never resolve.
- When no separate photo is found, the analyzer clearly reports it and immediately continues to the next recipe.
- Recipe text extraction and import continue normally even when a cookbook has flattened or inaccessible images.
