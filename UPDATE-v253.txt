BUILD 253 — Durable planner recovery and serialized sync

Fixes:
- Keeps a durable recovery journal that cannot be replaced by an older five-meal shared copy.
- Restores the fullest local planner copy before shared synchronization starts.
- Does not rotate a fuller backup out when a stale/partial plan appears.
- Automatically retries a failed user save without requiring a refresh.
- Serializes Apps Script meal-plan writes with LockService to prevent revision races.
