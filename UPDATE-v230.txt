Recipe Vault Build 230 — Cookbook Re-import Ownership Recovery

Install this build instead of Builds 228 or 229.

What changed
- The uploaded cookbook PDF is treated as the source of truth during duplicate review.
- A strong duplicate currently attached to another cookbook is preselected as “Move existing recipe here.”
- The existing recipe is updated and reassigned instead of creating a second copy.
- Nothing moves until you confirm the full import batch by clicking Import selected.
- Keep both and Skip remain available for genuinely ambiguous cases.
- Cookbook recipe indexes are rebuilt after confirmed moves so reclaimed recipes are removed from the wrong cookbook.
- No background scanner automatically changes cookbook ownership.
