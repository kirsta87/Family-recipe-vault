RECIPE VAULT v1.5.1 — COOKBOOK PHOTO IMPORT

NEW
- Scans recipe pages for embedded cookbook photography.
- Matches the largest likely food photo to each detected recipe.
- Shows each detected photo in the cookbook review queue.
- Lets you include or exclude the photo per recipe before import.
- Imports approved photos into the recipe image field.
- Displays imported photos while browsing a cookbook.

IMPORTANT
- Photo extraction is best-effort because cookbook PDFs are built differently.
- PDFs that flatten a full page into one image may not expose the recipe photo separately.
- The review queue will clearly say when no separate photo was found.
- Photos are resized and compressed so they can travel through the existing Recipe Vault write workflow.
